We currently support the following event types.

Revision events (`*.revision.*`) carry the revision's ID, version and base version, status, title, description, dates, author, schedule and the entity it belongs to, plus the event's own fields such as the reviewer. They also carry its content: for Configs, Constants, Saved Groups and SDK Connections, the entity before and after the draft (`baseConfig` and `proposedConfig`, for example), the patch it applies (`proposedChanges`) and its reviews; for Feature Flags, the draft's default value, rules, prerequisites and other settings. SDK Connection revision events also carry the connection's webhooks before and after the draft (`baseSdkWebhooks` and `proposedSdkWebhooks`), without their headers. Feature Flag reviews, and the history of every revision, are on the [REST API](/api). Feature Flag revision events also keep the deprecated `featureId`, `date`, `createdBy` and `publishedBy` fields for existing integrations.
